Speak Up. We're Listening.

A confidential channel for reporting fraud, misconduct, safety issues or any other concern about Tor Delivery. You can report anonymously — no login, no account, no questions about who you are.

You are protected when you speak up

No one will face dismissal, demotion, or any other detriment for raising a genuine concern in good faith — regardless of whether the concern turns out to be substantiated. Retaliation against anyone who reports is itself a serious disciplinary matter.

About this channel

This channel exists so that concerns can be raised safely and acted on properly. It is open to anyone connected to Tor Delivery:

Employees Riders Merchants Customers Contractors

You can use it to report fraud or financial misconduct, theft, safety violations, harassment or discrimination, bribery and corruption, data privacy breaches, regulatory non-compliance, conflicts of interest, or any other concern about how Tor Delivery operates.

Reports go directly to the General Managing Director. Managers, supervisors and other superiors do not see them. Reports are not routed through line management or HR, and they are not visible to your supervisor or team.

If you would rather not use this form, you can email admin@tordelivery.com directly. Please note an email will show your address — use the anonymous option below if you would prefer we not know who you are.
