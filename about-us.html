Built to Move Nigeria's Businesses Forward

Tor Delivery started with one problem to solve: logistics in Cross River and Akwa Ibom was unreliable, untracked, and left business owners carrying the risk. We built the system we wished existed.

Hero image: Tor Delivery riders and team members, Calabar operations hub
Our Story

Built From a Real Operational Gap

Vendors were losing money to riders who disappeared with cash. Businesses were absorbing the cost of private fleets they barely used. Customers were waiting on deliveries nobody could track.

Tor Delivery exists to close that gap. We built a logistics network with vetted riders, tracked deliveries, and same-day remittance — infrastructure, not guesswork. What started as a single-city operation now serves individuals, SMEs, e-commerce brands, and corporate accounts across Cross River and Akwa Ibom States.

Image: dispatch team coordinating rider assignments at the operations desk
What We Stand For

The Principles Behind Every Delivery

Three commitments that shape how we operate, every day.

Accountability

Every rider is vetted. Every parcel is tracked. Every cash collection is logged against a named rider in real time. Nothing moves unaccounted for.

Speed

A delivery delayed is a customer lost. We built our dispatch system around same-day windows as standard, not as an upgrade you pay extra for.

Trust

Vendors trust us with their cash. Businesses trust us with their customers. We treat both as a standard we cannot afford to drop.
