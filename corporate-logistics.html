Chain of Custody

Auditable Supply Chain and Secure Freight Forwarding

Every corporate consignment is logged at collection, tracked in transit, and verified at the point of delivery. Our riders are vetted to a standard appropriate for sensitive cargo, and every handoff is recorded against a named recipient.

Documents and high-value goods receive a signed confirmation on arrival. You retain a complete record of custody from your dispatch desk to the recipient’s hand, available for your own compliance and audit requirements.

Capabilities

Enterprise Logistics Capabilities

Three capabilities built for organisations that cannot afford uncertainty.

Secure Document Transit

Built for law firms and financial institutions. Every document transit is handled under strict confidentiality protocol, with controlled access from collection to delivery.

High Volume Freight Distribution

Built for bulk goods distributors operating across Cross River and Akwa Ibom. We coordinate multi-point distribution runs at a scale a single courier cannot manage.

Dedicated Account Management

Your organisation is assigned a single point of contact for every shipment, every query, and every resolution. Disputes are addressed directly through that contact, not through a general enquiry line.

Bespoke Contracts

Custom Service Level Agreements

Standard pricing does not fit enterprise operations.

We build bespoke contracts around your organisation's actual requirements: defined delivery windows, agreed response times, and deferred monthly invoicing in place of per-shipment payment.

Your agreement is negotiated once and applied consistently, so your logistics costs are predictable and your operations team is not managing invoices shipment by shipment.

What your SLA can include

Defined delivery windows, agreed response times for urgent dispatch, deferred monthly invoicing, and a dedicated account contact named in your contract.
